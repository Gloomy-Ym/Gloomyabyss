I finally got around to doing something I've meant to do for a while. You see, when I first made these fonts, I was in my Roaring 20s, a scamp and a ne'er-do-well, and I gave many of my fonts Horny Names. Now I am in my Reflective 40s, at peace with myself and the world around me, and kind of worried that super horny names might put some people off from using these fonts?

So I've come up with some new names. Here are all the replacement names:

  • Chubster
  • Floatpoints
  • Ghost Butler
  • Jaywalk
  • Planet Gemrule
  • Trouble Beneath the Dome

They're still free to use, etc etc. Enjoy!
